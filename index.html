Valeaxia Uploader

The world, pieced together. Valeaxia makes short videos about war, money and power. Valeaxia Uploader is the channel's publishing tool for posting those videos to the Valeaxia TikTok account.

How it works

  1. Open Valeaxia Uploader and press Continue with TikTok. Sign in and approve access.
  2. Pick a video from the Valeaxia library or from your computer, and watch the preview.
  3. Write the caption, choose who can view it, and set comments, Duet, Stitch and content disclosure.
  4. Press Post to TikTok. The video appears on your profile within a few minutes.
