Privacy Policy — Valeaxia Uploader

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Valeaxia Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Valeaxia channel to publish its own videos to its own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

With the account owner's permission through TikTok Login Kit, the tool accesses basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) and the permission to upload and publish videos to that account.

How data is used

The data is used only to upload and publish the owner's videos. Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Tokens can be revoked at any time from TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions). Revoking access and deleting the local token file removes all stored data.

Contact

For questions, contact the Valeaxia channel through its YouTube or TikTok profile.
